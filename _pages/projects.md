## Appskep — iOS Nursing Exam Learning App

**Role:** UI/UX Designer & iOS Developer  
**Tech Stack:** SwiftUI (iOS), Golang backend, React-based dashboards, Midtrans payment gateway

- Mobile learning app for Indonesian nursing students preparing for UKOM (Nursing Competency Exam).
- Features: tryout questions, explanations, AI chatbot, AI scoring, and subscription/payments integration.
- Directed the design research and development cycle applying User-Centered Design (UCD) methodologies.
- Engineered a standardized Design System that improved SDLC efficiency by 50%.
- Designed data-driven interfaces for a platform serving 200,000+ users.
