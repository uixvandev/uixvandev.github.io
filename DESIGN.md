# DESIGN.md

Direction for the personal site of Irfan Wahendra. Written from the owner's answers.

## Identity
- Personal academic and product portfolio: Information Systems, mobile agentic AI, human-AI interaction, CTO at Teamly.
- Visual reference: a grid ground with glass panels, sticker tags, and a pixel name. Owner's sheet.

## Palette
- Navy and white, clean. Owner preference.
- Core: navy. Neutrals: white and cool grey. One accent: blue, used sparingly (name outline, link hover, focus).
- Light and dark modes both supported.
- Dark mode accent: #00D0FF (owner choice, replaces the light blue).

## Typography
- Text: Bricolage Grotesque.
- Name: Silkscreen (pixel), as in the owner's reference.
- Handwritten notes: Caveat.

## Material
- Glass panels with 3D depth, glossy top-left sheen. Owner keeps glass despite the dose cap.
- Grid background kept from the owner's reference.

## Dials
- ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.
- ENERGY 3: pixel name, sticker tags, bold glass.
- RHYTHM 2: sections vary: some split heading-left and content-right, others full width.
- MOTION 1: hover states and press feedback only. No loops.

## Owner decisions
- Keep glass and shadow on panels (overrides the 1 to 2 element dose cap).
- Keep grid background.
- Em dashes removed from owner copy, replaced with commas and colons.
- Ambient cursor icon roams the hero on a loop (owner request). This overrides MOTION 1 for that one element. It stops for reduced motion.
